## Field: overviewShort

**Survodutide (BI 456906)** is an investigational once-weekly **glucagon / GLP-1 receptor dual agonist** co-developed by Boehringer Ingelheim and Zealand Pharma for obesity and MASH/MASLD programs. **Not FDA-approved** as of this draft’s sources. Educational only—not medical advice.

## Field: overview

Survodutide activates both GLP-1 and glucagon receptors, a dual-agonist design intended to combine satiety/weight effects with hepatic metabolic actions relevant to steatotic liver disease. Phase 3 SYNCHRONIZE obesity/MASLD readouts and LIVERAGE MASH programs have been publicly discussed by the sponsor; research-chemical vials claiming “survodutide” are not clinical-trial or approved drug products.

## Field: background

Zealand/Boehringer dual-agonist collaboration; development codes include BI 456906. FDA Fast Track (2021) and Breakthrough Therapy (2024) designations have been reported for MASH-related development—these are **not approvals**.
