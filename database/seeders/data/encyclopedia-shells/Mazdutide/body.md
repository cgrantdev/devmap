## Paste-ready body

## Field: overviewShort

**Mazdutide** (also IBI362 / LY3305677 lineage) is a once-weekly **glucagon (GCG) / GLP-1 receptor dual agonist**. China’s **NMPA** has approved it (brand **Xinermei** / 信尔美 in Innovent communications) for chronic weight management and for glycemic control in adults with type 2 diabetes. It is **not FDA-approved**. Educational only—not medical advice.

## Field: overview

Mazdutide was discovered in Eli Lilly’s dual-agonist programs and licensed to **Innovent Biologics** for development and commercialization in China. Phase 3 programs (including GLORY weight-management and DREAMS glycemic series) supported Chinese approvals. U.S. development/publication activity has included Phase 2 work; as of this draft’s sources, **no U.S. FDA approval** was identified. Research-chemical “mazdutide” powders sold online are **not** the approved Chinese medicinal product.

## Field: background

Dual GCG/GLP-1 agonism aims to combine GLP-1–class satiety/glycemic effects with glucagon-receptor metabolic actions (including hepatic themes). Mazdutide is often discussed alongside other multi-agonists (survodutide, retatrutide)—each has a distinct molecule, sponsor, and regulatory path.

## Field: mechanismOfActionIntro

Peptide dual agonism at GLP-1R and GCGR—class related to, but not identical with, single GLP-1 RAs (semaglutide/tirzepatide are different molecules; tirzepatide is GIP/GLP-1).
