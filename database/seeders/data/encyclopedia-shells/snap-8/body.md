## Paste-ready body

## Field: overviewShort

**SNAP-8** is the cosmetic INCI-associated peptide **acetyl octapeptide-3**, an elongated SNAP-25–related analog marketed to reduce expression-line appearance. Evidence is largely **cosmetic/in-house or multi-ingredient**; **peer-reviewed trials isolating SNAP-8 alone are scarce**. Not an FDA-approved drug. Educational only.

## Field: overview

SNAP-8 extends the “Botox-like peptide” cosmetic narrative associated with shorter argireline-class peptides (acetyl hexapeptide-8), adding two amino acids. It appears in topical serums and, separately, in research-peptide catalogs as lyophilized powder. Those channels are not interchangeable evidence bases. Peptidemap lists vendor research products under this slug.

## Field: background

Cosmetic peptide houses popularized SNARE-complex–interfering peptide stories for topical wrinkle appearance. SNAP-8 branding is commercial; the INCI name acetyl octapeptide-3 is the clearer identity key for ingredient lists.

## Field: mechanismOfActionIntro

Proposed topical mechanism: modulation of SNARE-mediated neurotransmitter vesicle docking narratives in vitro—extrapolated to reduced expression-line depth cosmetically. This is not equivalent to approved neuromodulator injections.
