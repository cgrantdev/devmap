## Field: overviewShort

**Adamax** is a marketplace name for an **N-acetylated, adamantane-modified Semax analog** (often styled Acetyl Semax Adamantane). Vendor claims of superior CNS delivery are **extrapolations**; **peer-reviewed Adamax-specific clinical trials were not identified**. Semax literature is not Adamax proof. Educational only.

## Field: overview

Adamax appears in research-peptide catalogs as a “next-gen Semax.” Chemically, adamantane conjugation and N-acetylation are known medicinal-chemistry strategies to alter stability or distribution of peptides—but without identity-specific published human trials, those strategies remain hypotheses for this brand/analog class. Encyclopedia honesty requires a thin-evidence posture.

## Field: background

Semax (ACTH(4-7)+PGP) has a Russian clinical tradition. Adamax branding attaches adamantane and acetyl modifications popularized in nootropic commerce. Sitemap also shows related “Adalank / Adamax” blend confusion—treat marketplace names carefully and confirm COA sequences.
