## Paste-ready body

## Field: overviewShort

**GHRP-2 (pralmorelin)** is a synthetic growth hormone–releasing peptide that agonizes the ghrelin receptor (GHS-R1a) to stimulate pituitary GH release. It is used in **Japan as a diagnostic agent** for GH deficiency; it is **not FDA-approved** as a U.S. therapeutic. Educational only—not medical advice.

## Field: overview

GHRP-2 belongs to the GHRP class of ghrelin-mimetic secretagogues developed from early enkephalin-analog GH secretagogue chemistry. Human pharmacology shows robust GH pulses in healthy subjects, which underpins the Japanese diagnostic test use. U.S. research-chemical and historical compounding interest does not equal FDA therapeutic approval. Peptidemap catalogs vendor research products under this slug.

## Field: background

Also known as KP-102 / pralmorelin. Japanese marketing as a diagnostic (GHRP Kaken lineage) contrasts with grey-market “performance” framing. Often stacked in commerce with GHRP-6 or GHRH analogs—combination marketing is not combination-trial proof.

## Field: mechanismOfActionIntro

GHS-R1a agonism at pituitary/hypothalamus drives GH secretion via pathways distinct from GHRH receptor agonism; co-administration with GHRH-pathway agents can be synergistic in physiology studies.

## Field: regulatoryImportantNote

**Regulatory literacy:** Removal from FDA Category 2 (or a favorable Pharmacy Compounding Advisory Committee vote) is **not** placement on the final 503A Bulks List and does **not** by itself authorize compounding—verify current FDA compounding pages.
