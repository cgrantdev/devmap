## Paste-ready body

## Field: overviewShort

**Melanotan-I (MT-1)** is the historical research name for the synthetic α-MSH analog later developed as **afamelanotide**. Afamelanotide is FDA-approved as **Scenesse**, a controlled-release **implant** for adults with erythropoietic protoporphyria (EPP). **Grey-market “Melanotan-I” powders/injections are not Scenesse.** Educational only—not medical advice.

## Field: overview

University of Arizona melanocortin programs in the 1980s–90s produced NDP-α-MSH (afamelanotide / Melanotan-I) as a more potent, protease-resistant α-MSH analog acting primarily via MC1R for eumelanin synthesis. Clinuvel developed the implant formulation approved for EPP phototoxicity. Concurrently, unregulated internet “tanning peptide” commerce sells materials labeled Melanotan-I that lack the approved implant’s quality system, indication, and administration setting.

## Field: background

Naming map: Melanotan-I ≈ afamelanotide (same peptide lineage) ≠ Melanotan-II (cyclic, nonselective analog with broader receptor activity and a different safety story). Early human tanning pharmacology studies in the 1990s preceded rare-disease photomedicine development.

## Field: humanUseIntro

Two lanes: (1) **Scenesse (afamelanotide implant)**—FDA-approved (2019) for adult EPP phototoxicity, administered by trained clinicians; (2) **unregulated Melanotan-I products**—not approved for tanning or any U.S. cosmetic indication; regulators internationally have warned against unlicensed melanotan products.

## Field: regulatoryImportantNote

Approved indication is narrow (EPP implant). Unapproved melanotan marketing has drawn FDA and international consumer warnings. **Regulatory literacy:** Removal from FDA Category 2 (or a favorable Pharmacy Compounding Advisory Committee vote) is **not** placement on the final 503A Bulks List and does **not** by itself authorize compounding—verify current FDA compounding pages.

## FAQs

**Is Melanotan-I legal/approved?** Scenesse afamelanotide implant is FDA-approved for EPP. Online MT-1 tanning products are not that approved product.

**Same as Melanotan-II?** No.

**Can I use Scenesse evidence for DIY powder?** No—different product/quality/indication.
