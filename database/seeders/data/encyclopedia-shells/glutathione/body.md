### overviewShort

**Glutathione (GSH)** is a ubiquitous endogenous tripeptide (**glutamate–cysteine–glycine**) central to cellular redox biology, detoxification, and antioxidant defense. It is sold in research and compounding contexts in multiple forms (reduced GSH, sometimes oxidized GSSG discussions). **Injectable glutathione is not an FDA-approved drug** for skin lightening or general wellness. Educational only.

### overview

Biochemically, glutathione is one of the most important intracellular thiols. Clinical and public interest spans IV “push” wellness marketing, respiratory research, and oncology-adjacent redox studies. Peptidemap catalogs vendor research products under this encyclopedia slug. Readers must separate **endogenous biochemistry** (extremely well established) from **therapeutic claims for supplemental/injectable products** (often weakly evidenced and quality-sensitive).

### background

Discovered in the early 20th century as a cellular reducing agent, glutathione participates in glutathione peroxidase/reductase cycles, conjugation reactions (GST enzymes), and maintenance of protein thiols. Oral bioavailability of intact GSH is debated; IV/IM research-chemical and compounded presentations appear in clinics despite lack of an approved NDA product for cosmetic claims.

### humanUseIntro

Human studies of glutathione supplementation exist across oral, inhaled, and IV routes with **mixed and indication-specific** results. There is **no FDA-approved injectable glutathione** for skin whitening. CDC/public-health communications have warned about adverse events linked to compounded injectable glutathione made with dietary-supplement-grade ingredients contaminated with endotoxin.
