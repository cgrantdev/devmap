## Paste-ready body

## Field: overviewShort

**GHRP-6** is a synthetic hexapeptide growth hormone secretagogue (His-D-Trp-Ala-Trp-D-Phe-Lys-NH2) that acts at the ghrelin receptor (GHS-R1a). It is **not FDA-approved**. FDA compounding safety-risk materials discuss immunogenicity/impurity and metabolic safety concerns. Educational only—not medical advice.

## Field: overview

GHRP-6 was among the first hexapeptides with substantial in vivo GH-releasing activity in the Bowers-era secretagogue lineage. It remains highly visible in research-peptide catalogs and historical compounding nominations (often with GHRP-2/sermorelin). Human pharmacology demonstrates GH release; cortisol and appetite effects are part of class discussions. No U.S. approved drug product supports marketplace “recovery/anti-aging” claims.

## Field: background

Parent hexapeptide of the GHRP series; later analogs (GHRP-2, hexarelin, ipamorelin) aimed to tune potency and side-effect profiles. Theratechnologies and others explored related secretagogue programs historically without yielding a modern U.S. GHRP-6 NDA for wellness indications.

## Field: mechanismOfActionIntro

GHS-R1a agonism stimulates GH; simultaneous GHRH-pathway stimulation can amplify pulses in physiology studies.

Human studies document GH secretory responses. There is **no FDA-approved GHRP-6 product**.
