## Field: overviewShort

**Semax** is a synthetic heptapeptide analog of ACTH(4-7) with a Pro-Gly-Pro C-terminal extension (Met-Glu-His-Phe-Pro-Gly-Pro). It is registered for certain neurologic indications in Russia; it is **not FDA-approved** in the United States. Educational only—not medical advice.

## Field: overview

Developed within Russian regulatory-peptide programs, Semax is studied and used clinically in that jurisdiction for cerebrovascular and neuroprotective contexts (including ischemic stroke rehabilitation research). U.S. research-chemical sales and “nootropic” marketing outrun the availability of large, independent Western RCTs. Peptidemap lists vendor research products under this slug.

## Field: background

Semax derives from ACTH fragment structure–activity work aimed at retaining neurotropic effects while reducing classical ACTH endocrine activity. Related Russian peptides include Selank (different parent motif). Naming in commerce is usually “Semax”; salt forms and concentrations on nasal/research products vary by vendor.

## Field: mechanismOfActionIntro

Leading research themes include neurotrophin expression (BDNF/NGF pathways), transcriptome shifts in ischemic brain models, and neuroprotective signaling—not a simple single-receptor consumer story.
