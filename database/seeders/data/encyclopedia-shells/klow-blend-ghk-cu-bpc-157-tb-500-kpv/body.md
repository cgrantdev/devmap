## Paste-ready body

## Field: overviewShort

**KLOW** is a **four-component marketplace blend name**. Peptidemap’s slug and live product naming encode **GHK-Cu + BPC-157 + TB-500 + KPV** (example product title observed: “KLOW — GHK-Cu/BPC-157/TB-500/KPV”). It is not a single molecule. **No published human study of this four-peptide combination** was identified. Educational only.

## Field: overview

KLOW extends the three-peptide **GLOW** idea by adding **KPV** (Lys-Pro-Val), the C-terminal tripeptide of α-MSH studied mainly for anti-inflammatory biology in preclinical models. Peptidemap groups many vendor SKUs under this encyclopedia slug (vendorCount high; body previously empty).

Some catalog cards show only “KLOW (80mg)” without breaking out milligrams per peptide—**lot COAs remain essential**. At least one mis-tagged listing observed in props used a KPV-only product name under the KLOW category feed—another reason to read individual product pages carefully.

## Field: background

Naming appears commercial (“K” + GLOW → KLOW) rather than pharmacopeial. The scientific task is to keep four literatures separate and to refuse blend efficacy claims without combination data.

## Field: preclinicalDisclaimer

Four preclinical stories ≠ one proven therapy. Combination toxicology and efficacy for KLOW are not established in published human trials.
