## What each product is

Orforglipron is an oral GLP-1 receptor agonist from Eli Lilly and Company. The ATTAIN-1 paper in the New England Journal of Medicine describes it as a small-molecule, nonpeptide GLP-1 receptor agonist. The US prescribing information for Foundayo, the brand name, describes the same active substance as an oral GLP-1 receptor agonist and gives a small-molecule chemical name. It does not describe a peptide backbone.

Oral semaglutide is Novo Nordisk's semaglutide in a tablet. The US prescribing information says the peptide backbone is produced by yeast fermentation and that the tablets are co-formulated with salcaprozate sodium, also called SNAC, which the label describes as an absorption enhancer. The label says absorption after oral administration occurs predominantly in the stomach.

In the United States, the diabetes oral-semaglutide label checked on 22 October 2026 covers two tablet products, Rybelsus and Ozempic tablets, and states that they are not substitutable for one another. The Wegovy label checked the same day includes tablets for oral use as well as the injection. In the European Union, Rybelsus has held a marketing authorisation for type 2 diabetes since 3 April 2020. The Wegovy EU authorisation includes tablets for adult weight management. The procedural-steps record lists a European Commission decision of 14 July 2026 for that tablet extension, after a CHMP positive opinion on 21 May 2026.

## How the molecules differ

Both act at the GLP-1 receptor. That shared target does not make them the same molecule, the same tablet, or interchangeable.

Orforglipron is not a peptide. A phase 2 paper in the New England Journal of Medicine calls it a nonpeptide GLP-1 receptor agonist, and ATTAIN-1 calls it a small-molecule, nonpeptide oral agonist. The Foundayo label describes a synthetic small molecule, not a chain of amino acids.

Oral semaglutide is a peptide analogue of GLP-1. The US label describes modifications that slow breakdown and support albumin binding, and it states that the tablets rely on SNAC for oral absorption. The Foundayo label does not list SNAC as an ingredient and does not describe an absorption enhancer.

A difference in molecule type is not a trial result. This page does not say that one mechanism is stronger, safer, or preferable.

## Regulatory status as of 22 October 2026

### United States

The FDA approved Foundayo (orforglipron) tablets on 1 April 2026. The approval announcement and the current label use the same weight-management indication: with a reduced-calorie diet and increased physical activity, to reduce excess body weight and maintain weight reduction long term in adults with obesity, or in adults with overweight and at least one weight-related comorbid condition. The label indication checked on 22 October 2026 is that use. It does not list type 2 diabetes.

Oral semaglutide is already an approved tablet in the United States under more than one brand. The Rybelsus and Ozempic tablet label indicates those tablets, with diet and exercise, to improve glycemic control in adults with type 2 diabetes, and to reduce the risk of major adverse cardiovascular events in adults with type 2 diabetes who are at high risk for those events. The Wegovy label indicates the tablets, with diet and increased physical activity, to reduce cardiovascular risk in adults with established cardiovascular disease and obesity or overweight, and for weight management in adults with obesity, or some adults with overweight and a weight-related condition. Those are separate labelled products. Approval of one does not approve the others, and none of them is an approval of a research-use powder or capsule.

### European Union

On 15 October 2026 the CHMP adopted a positive opinion recommending a marketing authorisation for Foundayo (orforglipron). The EMA product page states that the opinion covers type 2 diabetes mellitus and weight management. Meeting highlights from the CHMP meeting of 12-15 October 2026, published on 16 October 2026, list Foundayo as pending a European Commission decision. The product page says the summary of product characteristics will be published after the Commission grants the authorisation. As of 22 October 2026, the positive opinion is not itself an EU marketing authorisation.

Rybelsus (semaglutide tablets) has been authorised in the EU for type 2 diabetes since 3 April 2020. Wegovy has been authorised in the EU since 6 January 2022. Its current EMA overview describes both a pre-filled pen and tablets, and it says the tablets are for adults. The procedural-steps document records the Commission decision of 14 July 2026 that added the tablet form.

## What the labels state about food and water

This section describes label conditions. It is not an instruction to take any tablet.

The US prescribing information for Rybelsus tablets and Ozempic tablets states that a tablet is taken on an empty stomach in the morning with water, up to 4 ounces, and not with other liquids, and that at least 30 minutes pass before food, beverages, or other oral medicines.

The US prescribing information for Wegovy tablets states the same kind of condition for the tablet: an empty stomach in the morning, water up to 4 ounces, no other liquids, and a wait of at least 30 minutes before food, beverages, or other oral medicines. The injection section of that label is a different product and is not this oral comparison.

The EMA overview of Wegovy states that the tablets are taken once a day on an empty stomach, after at least 8 hours of fasting, and that 30 minutes pass before eating, drinking, or taking other medicines. That EU wording is not the same sentence as the US label. This page does not merge the two texts into one rule.

The Foundayo US label states that the tablet is taken orally once daily, with or without food. It does not state an empty-stomach condition, and it does not state a maximum volume of water. In the phase 2 trial, orforglipron or matching placebo was given once daily by mouth in the morning without meal-timing restrictions. Lilly's press release of 1 April 2026, which is a company announcement, describes Foundayo as a tablet that can be taken any time of day without restrictions on food and water intake. The company sentence is broader than the administration sentence in the label. Both are cited below. Neither is advice from Peptidemap.

## What the trials studied

The notes below are design and population only. They do not print per-arm weight-change percentages, they do not name dose arms by milligram, and they do not rank one trial against another. The trials differ in who was enrolled, how long treatment lasted, and whether the comparator was placebo or another drug.

### Orforglipron: ATTAIN and ACHIEVE

ATTAIN-1 (NCT05869903) is a phase 3, randomized, double-blind trial sponsored by Eli Lilly. It assigned adults with obesity, or with overweight and weight-related comorbidities, and without diabetes, to once-daily orforglipron in one of three dose arms or to placebo for 72 weeks, with diet and physical activity. The New England Journal of Medicine paper reports that 3,127 participants were randomized. The primary endpoint was percent change in body weight. The percentages are in the paper. They are not repeated here.

ACHIEVE-3 (NCT06045221) is a phase 3, open-label, randomized, non-inferiority trial in adults with type 2 diabetes treated with metformin. It compared once-daily orforglipron dose arms with once-daily oral semaglutide dose arms for 52 weeks. The Lancet paper reports 1,698 participants. This is a head-to-head trial in type 2 diabetes. It is not a trial in people with obesity and no diabetes, and it is not ATTAIN-1. Outcome figures from ACHIEVE-3, with the estimand named, are on the [orforglipron versus oral semaglutide blog](/blog/orforglipron-vs-oral-semaglutide). This page does not copy them.

A phase 2 trial (NCT05051579), also in the New England Journal of Medicine, enrolled adults with obesity, or with overweight and at least one weight-related condition, without diabetes, and assigned once-daily orforglipron dose arms or placebo for 36 weeks. It is an earlier study, not ATTAIN-1. Its weight-change percentages are not repeated here.

### Oral semaglutide: PIONEER and OASIS

PIONEER 6 (NCT02692716) is one trial in the PIONEER programme of oral semaglutide for type 2 diabetes. The New England Journal of Medicine paper describes it as an event-driven, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled cardiovascular outcomes trial. Participants were adults with type 2 diabetes at high cardiovascular risk: age 50 or older with established cardiovascular or chronic kidney disease, or age 60 or older with cardiovascular risk factors only. They were assigned to once-daily oral semaglutide or placebo, added to standard care. This page does not restate event counts from that paper. Other PIONEER trials are not summarised here.

OASIS 1 (NCT05035095) is a phase 3, randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial sponsored by Novo Nordisk. It enrolled adults with overweight or obesity without type 2 diabetes and assigned once-daily oral semaglutide or placebo for 68 weeks, with a lifestyle intervention. The registry lists 667 participants randomized. The Lancet paper reports the weight results. Those percentages are not printed here.

OASIS 4 (NCT05564117) is a separate phase 3, randomized, double-blind trial of once-daily oral semaglutide versus placebo in adults with overweight or obesity. The registry lists 307 participants enrolled and a 64-week treatment period. It is not OASIS 1, and it is not ACHIEVE-3.

ACHIEVE-3 is the trial in this set that assigned orforglipron and oral semaglutide in the same study. ATTAIN-1, PIONEER 6, OASIS 1, and OASIS 4 did not. A result from one of those trials is not a result for the other drug, and a figure in one paper is not a ranking against another paper.

## FAQ

### Is orforglipron a peptide?

No. The ATTAIN-1 paper in the New England Journal of Medicine describes orforglipron as a small-molecule, nonpeptide oral GLP-1 receptor agonist, and a phase 2 paper in the same journal calls it a nonpeptide GLP-1 receptor agonist. Oral semaglutide is a peptide. The US label says its peptide backbone is produced by yeast fermentation and that the tablets contain salcaprozate sodium (SNAC), an absorption enhancer.

### What is oral semaglutide on the current labels?

Oral semaglutide is Novo Nordisk's peptide GLP-1 receptor agonist in a tablet. On the US labels checked on 22 October 2026, Rybelsus tablets and Ozempic tablets are indicated for type 2 diabetes, and Wegovy tablets are indicated for adult weight management and for cardiovascular risk reduction in adults with established cardiovascular disease and obesity or overweight. In the EU, Rybelsus has been authorised for type 2 diabetes since 3 April 2020, and the Wegovy authorisation includes tablets for adult weight management.

### Is Foundayo approved in the United States and the European Union as of 22 October 2026?

In the United States, yes, for weight management. The FDA approved Foundayo (orforglipron) tablets on 1 April 2026 for adults with obesity, or overweight with at least one weight-related condition, together with diet and activity. The US label indication checked on 22 October 2026 is that use. It does not list type 2 diabetes. In the European Union, not yet as a granted authorisation. The CHMP adopted a positive opinion on 15 October 2026 recommending a marketing authorisation for type 2 diabetes and weight management. EMA's meeting highlights, published on 16 October 2026, list that opinion as pending a European Commission decision. A positive opinion is not a granted EU authorisation.

### How do the labels differ on food and water?

The US labels for Rybelsus tablets, Ozempic tablets, and Wegovy tablets state an empty-stomach morning condition, water up to 4 ounces, no other liquids, and a wait of at least 30 minutes before food, drink, or other oral medicines. Those sentences are label conditions, not instructions from this page. The Foundayo label states that the tablet is taken once daily with or without food, and it does not state an empty-stomach condition or a water-volume limit. A phase 2 trial gave orforglipron once daily without meal-timing restrictions. Lilly's 1 April 2026 press release, which is company-reported, says Foundayo can be taken any time of day without food or water restrictions.

### Did a trial compare orforglipron with oral semaglutide, and does this page rank the programmes?

ACHIEVE-3 compared them in adults with type 2 diabetes who were taking metformin. It was a 52-week, open-label, randomized phase 3 trial. This page does not repeat its outcome figures. Those figures, with the estimand labelled, are on the orforglipron versus oral semaglutide blog. ATTAIN-1, PIONEER 6, OASIS 1, and OASIS 4 enrolled different populations and used different designs. This page does not rank one programme against another.

### Is a research-use listing the same as the approved tablet?

No. A research-use-only product sold under one of these names is not Foundayo, not Rybelsus, not an Ozempic tablet, and not a Wegovy tablet.
