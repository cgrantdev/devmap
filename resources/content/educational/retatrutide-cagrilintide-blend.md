Some research catalogs sell a product named as retatrutide plus cagrilintide in one vial. That product name is a catalog label. It is not a published clinical trial, and it is not a registrational program name.

## Two molecules, two sponsors, two programs

Retatrutide (LY3437943) is Eli Lilly’s investigational once-weekly agonist at the GIP, GLP-1, and glucagon receptors. Its published phase 3 obesity program is TRIUMPH. The TRIUMPH papers cited here study retatrutide alone, not retatrutide co-formulated with cagrilintide.

Cagrilintide is Novo Nordisk’s long-acting amylin analogue. Its published phase 3 program is REDEFINE. The combination Novo calls CagriSema is cagrilintide with semaglutide. REDEFINE-1 also includes a cagrilintide-alone arm. Neither CagriSema nor that monotherapy arm is a retatrutide blend.

Putting the two molecules in one research vial is not CagriSema and not TRIUMPH. A shared shop category does not create a head-to-head, a co-primary endpoint, or a blend efficacy percent.

## TRIUMPH-1: retatrutide alone, no diabetes

TRIUMPH-1 (NCT05929066) is an 80-week phase 3 trial in adults with obesity or overweight without type 2 diabetes. Jastreboff and colleagues published it in the *New England Journal of Medicine* (DOI 10.1056/NEJMoa2604169). Participants were assigned to once-weekly subcutaneous retatrutide at 4 mg, 9 mg, or 12 mg, or placebo.

Weight end points used a treatment-regimen estimand (intention-to-treat style). At week 80 the estimated mean percent change in body weight was −17.6% at 4 mg, −23.7% at 9 mg, and −25.0% at 12 mg, versus −3.9% with placebo. The same dataset’s efficacy estimand was −19.0% at 4 mg, −25.9% at 9 mg, and −28.3% at 12 mg. Those rows are retatrutide monotherapy. They are not a blend result.

## TRIUMPH-2: retatrutide alone, with type 2 diabetes

TRIUMPH-2 (NCT05929079; Bellido et al., *Lancet*, DOI 10.1016/S0140-6736(26)01861-1) enrolled adults with obesity or overweight and type 2 diabetes for 80 weeks of once-weekly retatrutide at 4 mg, 9 mg, or 12 mg, or placebo.

Under the treatment-regimen estimand, mean percent change in body weight at week 80 was −11.9% at 4 mg, −16.8% at 9 mg, and −18.8% at 12 mg, versus −5.1% with placebo. Under the efficacy estimand the same arms were −12.7%, −19.1%, and −20.8%, versus −4.0% with placebo. These figures are still retatrutide alone. They cannot be moved onto a catalog blend or onto CagriSema.

## REDEFINE-1: CagriSema and cagrilintide alone

REDEFINE-1 (NCT05567796) is a 68-week phase 3a trial in adults with overweight or obesity without diabetes. Garvey and colleagues published it in the *New England Journal of Medicine* (DOI 10.1056/NEJMoa2502081). Arms were once-weekly cagrilintide 2.4 mg plus semaglutide 2.4 mg (CagriSema), semaglutide 2.4 mg alone, cagrilintide 2.4 mg alone, or placebo, plus lifestyle intervention for all groups.

The coprimary analysis uses the treatment-policy estimand. At week 68 the estimated mean percent change in body weight was −20.4% with CagriSema, −14.9% with semaglutide alone, −11.5% with cagrilintide alone, and −3.0% with placebo. The trial-product estimand in the same paper was −22.7% for CagriSema, −16.1% for semaglutide, −11.8% for cagrilintide alone, and −2.3% for placebo.

The −20.4% and −22.7% figures are CagriSema—cagrilintide with semaglutide. The cagrilintide-only arms are −11.5% (treatment-policy) and −11.8% (trial-product). None of those percents is a retatrutide–cagrilintide blend.

## REDEFINE-2: CagriSema with type 2 diabetes

REDEFINE-2 (NCT05394519; Davies et al., *NEJM*, DOI 10.1056/NEJMoa2502082) compared once-weekly CagriSema with placebo for 68 weeks in adults with overweight or obesity and type 2 diabetes. It did not include a cagrilintide-only arm and did not include retatrutide.

Under the treatment-policy estimand, estimated mean change in body weight at week 68 was −13.7% with CagriSema and −3.4% with placebo. That −13.7% is combination versus placebo only. It is not cagrilintide monotherapy and not a retatrutide blend.

## What TRIUMPH-5 actually says

ClinicalTrials.gov NCT06662383 is titled TRIUMPH-5. The official title is a phase 3, randomized, double-blind study of the efficacy and safety of retatrutide compared with tirzepatide in adults who have obesity. Status fetched on 5 October 2026: ACTIVE_NOT_RECRUITING. Estimated enrollment: 800. Arms listed: retatrutide (experimental) and tirzepatide (active comparator). Primary outcome: percent change from baseline in body weight at week 80. The record does not list cagrilintide as an intervention. HasResults was false when checked. This note invents no outcome percent for TRIUMPH-5.

A ClinicalTrials.gov search for both “retatrutide” and “cagrilintide” returned no registered study of that pairing on the same date. Catalog co-vialing is not a substitute for a registered combination trial.

## Why these percents cannot be stacked onto a blend

A larger percent is not evidence for a premixed research vial. The studies differ on the points that move a result:

- Molecule and partner. TRIUMPH uses retatrutide alone. REDEFINE’s combination is cagrilintide with semaglutide. Neither program published a retatrutide–cagrilintide co-formulation result.
- Population. TRIUMPH-1 and REDEFINE-1 excluded type 2 diabetes. TRIUMPH-2 and REDEFINE-2 required it.
- Time. TRIUMPH weight endpoints are at 80 weeks. REDEFINE endpoints are at 68 weeks.
- Estimand. Treatment-regimen or treatment-policy rows are not the same as efficacy or trial-product rows. A combination percent is not monotherapy.
